# zelmik-app

Support

Need help with Zelmik? Email us at sozensoftware@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device? Open the app → tap the Grown-ups button → pass the parental gate → open Zelmik Club → tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone/iPad Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Zelmik. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
What is the parental gate? Purchases, links and settings ask for the parent's birth year so children can't open them on their own.
How do I reset the game? Grown-ups Area → Reset progress. Stars, gems and garage items are erased; the subscription is not affected.
Is any data collected about my child? No. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy below.
